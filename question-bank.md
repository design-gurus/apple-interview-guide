# Apple Interview Question Bank

This page lists 58 practice questions for the Apple loop: 32 coding, 14 system design, and 12 behavioral. The coding questions cover the topics that candidates commonly report, and each one links to a lesson that solves it. Apple teams write their own questions, so use this list to practice the patterns and not to predict exact questions.

In the tables, GTCI means Grokking the Coding Interview and G75 means Grokking 75: Top Coding Interview Questions.

### Reported design-style coding questions

These two questions are commonly reported for Apple and have no single lesson.

- Build a thread-safe LRU cache with constant-time reads and writes. An LRU cache removes the least recently used item when it is full.
- Implement a hash map, then explain how it grows.

## System design

1. Design a cloud file sync service like iCloud.
2. Design a messaging system like iMessage that works across a phone, a tablet, a laptop, and a watch.
3. Design a media streaming service like Apple Music or Apple TV+.
4. Design push notifications for phones.
5. Design a mobile payment service like Apple Pay.
6. Design a photo storage and sharing service like Apple Photos.
7. Design a device backup system.
8. Design an online store.
9. Design a recommendation system for the App Store.
10. Design a search engine for content on the device.
11. Design a health monitoring system that collects data from a watch.
12. Design a notification system that reaches millions of users in real time.
13. iOS: design a note that one person edits on two devices.
14. iOS: design a feature that must keep working with no network.

## Behavioral

1. Why Apple?
2. What is your favorite Apple product, and how would you improve it?
3. Tell me about a product you helped build that you are proud of.
4. Describe the project where you paid the most attention to detail.
5. Tell me about a time you opposed shipping something because the quality was not there.
6. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a design or technical decision.
7. Tell me about a decision you made with significant ambiguity.
8. Tell me about a time you failed. What changed afterward?
9. Describe a challenging moment in your career.
10. Tell me about a time you convinced someone when you had no authority over them.
11. Describe a serious disagreement with a colleague or manager.
12. Tell me about working with a team whose priorities conflicted with yours.
